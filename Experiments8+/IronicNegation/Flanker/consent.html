Study Information & Consent

Eberhard Karls University Tübingen | Department of Psychology
Language and Cognition Research Group

Responsible contact person for queries:
Language & Cognition Lab (cognition-studies@uni-tuebingen.de)

Study Procedure & Tasks: This is a computer-based cognitive experiment. During this study, you will respond to the direction of a central arrow surrounded by flanking arrows using keyboard keys ('Q' and 'P'). Total duration is approximately 25–27 minutes.

Voluntary Participation and Anonymity:
Participation in this study is completely voluntary. You can decide to terminate your participation at any time and without giving reasons, with no negative consequences. All research data are stored completely anonymously and cannot be traced back to your individual identity.

Data Protection:
Data collection is strictly anonymous; we will never record your name or personal identifiers. The research data will be stored securely on protected servers at the University of Tübingen and will be processed in accordance with General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) standards. Anonymised data will be shared with the scientific community via open-science repositories to ensure reproducibility.

Remuneration (Prolific):
Participants recruited via Prolific will receive €5.85 (pro-rated at €13.00/hour) upon completion. Please follow the completion link provided at the end of the experiment to record your submission.
